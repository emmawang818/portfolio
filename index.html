ABOUT

Hiiiiiiiii, glad you’re here!

I’m Emma Wang, a UX/UI designer with three years of experience and a master’s in Interaction and Experience Design from Jiangnan University. At Ruijie Networks, I design for users worldwide.

My work spans enterprise platforms, mobile apps, and conversational AI. I simplify complex workflows into clear, intuitive experiences that help people get things done.

I believe good design starts with understanding people—balancing visual clarity and usability to solve real problems.
